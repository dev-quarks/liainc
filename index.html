About LIA Procurement Inc.

Indigenous-Led Procurement Excellence

LIA Procurement Inc. is a 100% Indigenous-owned firm working across federal, provincial, and municipal procurement in Canada. We engage directly in public tenders and government solicitations — sometimes as prime contractor, sometimes as strategic partner behind the scenes.

"We built this firm to answer the challenges our communities have long faced — grounded in trust, in listening, and in moving forward together, one partnership at a time."

Every engagement is shaped around fit: the right expertise matched to the right opportunity, guided by respect, reciprocity, and relationship.
