Where we work

Four sectors. Deep public sector experience in each.

Government buyers evaluate suppliers on technical fit, compliance, and delivery track record. We focus on four categories where we know the requirements, the buyers, and the evaluation criteria.

01

Transportation

Support for public works and mobility projects from opportunity identification through contract delivery.

  • Roads, bridges, and transit infrastructure
  • Fleet, logistics, and maintenance services
  • Planning, engineering, and advisory services
02

Energy

Procurement support for generation, transmission, and utility programs where reliability and compliance are paramount.

  • Generation and renewable energy projects
  • Grid modernization and utility services
  • Energy efficiency and retrofit programs
03

Security

Bids for physical, operational, and personnel security services and the systems that support them.

  • Physical security and access control
  • Monitoring and response services
  • Security assessment and compliance
04

Equipment & Technology

Supply and integration of equipment, software, and digital services for government operations.

  • Equipment supply and lifecycle management
  • Software, IT infrastructure, and cloud services
  • Digital transformation and data advisory
What we do

Support across the full procurement lifecycle.

Whether you are bidding for the first time or managing an active contract, we work alongside your team at each stage.

01

Procurement Strategy

We identify the right opportunities, assess fit and competition, and build a plan for which solicitations to pursue and how to position for each one.

02

Bid Preparation

Proposal development, technical writing, compliance review, and submission management, structured to answer the evaluation criteria directly.

03

Contract Management

Post-award support covering delivery milestones, compliance reporting, and client communication, so contracts stay on track and relationships hold.

04

Business Development

Market mapping, buyer relationship planning, and teaming arrangements that help you build a sustained public sector pipeline.

How we work

A disciplined approach to every solicitation.

Step 01

Assess

We confirm that an opportunity matches your capabilities, past performance, and commercial goals before any effort goes into a bid.

Step 02

Prepare

We build a compliant, evaluation-focused submission and review it against the solicitation requirements before it goes out.

Step 03

Deliver

After award, we help manage performance, reporting, and stakeholder communication so that the contract builds your reputation.

About LIA

LIA Procurement Inc. is a Canadian procurement firm focused on the public sector. Our work is built on a clear understanding of how government buyers evaluate suppliers and what it takes to deliver on a contract.

We work with federal, provincial, and municipal clients and with the suppliers who want to serve them. Our clients range from established firms entering new markets to organizations pursuing their first government contract.

Every engagement starts with fit. We match the right expertise to the right opportunity and stay involved through delivery.
